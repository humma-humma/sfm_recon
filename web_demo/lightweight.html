GNT and 2DGS: actual research implementations

Visual review decision: 2DGS is rejected for all four datasets because its trial renders are unusable for this project. Its results remain here as an experiment archive. GNT improves scene indoors; outdoors and static objects are approximately on par with the preferred restoration, with possible outdoor detail loss. Retain the preferred restoration for those cases.

Choose any two methods. Both share the same crop and camera. The original render is the default on the right. Zoom and drag to inspect fine details; select the held-out photograph to see the remaining gap. Motion entries are three coarse positions at half resolution, without a reference photograph.

Previously preferred restoration means prior on original for box, boot and milk; surface/photo plus learned prior for scene.



GNT: official pretrained generalization model, no fine-tuning. 2DGS: official optimizer and rasterizer, 10,000-step geometry-regularized pilot. These are limited trials, not claims of full paper reproduction or convergence. RGB targets are held out, but camera/seed estimation predates the split. Times include shared GPU contention.
