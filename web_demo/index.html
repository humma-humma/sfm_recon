From photographs to navigable 3D.

This project reconstructs tabletop objects and a building from photographs and RGB-D video. It combines classical structure from motion, bundle adjustment and RGB-D SLAM with learned loop closure, alongside neural view synthesis and Gaussian splatting to turn captured camera routes into interactive visual tours.

Reading the metrics: PSNR measures pixel fidelity in decibels; SSIM measures structural similarity. Higher is better for both, but neither fully captures perceived detail or temporal smoothness.
