GNT anchors with intermediate splats

Same outdoor turning clip, 960 × 540 at 30 fps. GNT supplies the anchor frames; the existing balanced 90/10 splat supplies every other frame at its exact recorded camera pose. No interpolation, optical-flow warping, crossfade, blending or held gap frames.

Choose 5, 10 or 15 GNT views/s in either candidate panel. Look for changes in detail or brightness as playback switches between GNT and splat frames. The same fixed splat model is used at all rates; this is a playback-composition test, not new training at those rates.

Scores and limitations

Gap-only scores measure the splat frames, without GNT anchors inflating the average. The shared gap comparison uses 58 frames that are splat-filled at all three rates. Those frames have the same source images at every rate. Overall scores also include GNT anchors. Agreement is measured against GNT, not photographs; the splat has already trained on some views in this clip. This outdoor test does not establish indoor/dynamic-scene quality.
