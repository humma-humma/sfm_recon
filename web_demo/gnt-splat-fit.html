GNT-guided splat fitting

Separate candidate models; existing selections are unchanged. Mixed fits combine 275 sparse GNT views with 241 original training photographs. GNT-only uses only the 275 rendered RGB targets. The geometry variant continues GNT-only for 2k steps using GNT RGB and confidence-filtered sensor depth from the 241 training cameras, with no original-photo RGB loss; its comparison also changes training duration. Pruning removes repeated free-space conflicts with no surface support. The dense GNT clip and 35 held-out photographs/depth maps are excluded from these losses. Inherited camera estimates and seed points predate the split. These are local Splatfacto experiments, not reproductions of ReSplat, DepthSplat or RadSplat. All splat videos render genuine camera views without motion interpolation.
