Measured-depth reconstruction experiment

A controlled adaptation of uncertainty-aware depth-prior ideas. Both splat models start from the same fresh 5k checkpoint and receive 5k additional iterations. Poses, images, initialization and resolution schedule are fixed. Only the candidate adds confidence-weighted sensor depth. All 35 held-out RGB and depth frames are excluded from the added loss, confidence cache and TSDF. The inherited sequence seed cloud was not rebuilt with a training-only split: this is a matched continuation ablation, not a strictly held-out end-to-end reconstruction benchmark.

Dense Depth Priors inspires the depth/confidence constraint; this is not its NeRF model or learned depth completion. The independent TSDF mesh uses estimated poses and training RGB-D; it is not a BundleFusion reproduction.

Left to right below: source photograph / RGB-only 10k control / depth-supervised 10k candidate.

RGB gate: passes_numeric_gate_visual_review_pending. Geometry checks: {'median_depth_error': True, 'pavement_preserved': True}. Selected presentation models remain unchanged.

Mean PSNR change: +0.586 dB; worst view: -0.383 dB. Median of six per-view depth errors: 0.606 → 0.473 m.
