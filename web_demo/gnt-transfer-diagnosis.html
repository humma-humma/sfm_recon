Where does GNT-to-splat transfer fail?

The frozen GNT-only 10k Gaussian model is compared with all 275 exact RGB training targets, 180 existing dense-turn targets and 48 existing adaptive targets. Views within 0.1 ms of a fitted timestamp are excluded from unseen summaries. No new GNT images or training were needed. Teacher agreement is not photographic ground truth.

Local gap compares an unseen pose with the interpolated mean-square error of its two neighboring fitted poses. A negative gap means worse agreement between training cameras. Different visible content and the deliberately difficult adaptive selection still confound this comparison; it does not prove a geometric cause. Dense frames cover one outdoor turn and are temporally correlated.
